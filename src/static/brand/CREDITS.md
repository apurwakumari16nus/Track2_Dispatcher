## Threadline backgrounds (V10)

Approved Threadline brand artwork supplied by the project team, in `src/static/threadline/backgrounds/`.
The originals are kept unchanged. Three transparent layers are derived from them (V10): each keeps only
the threads (colour-to-alpha against the asset's own ground, with flat compression haze removed) and fades
to zero alpha before every edge, so no layer can show a rectangle. They are resized uniformly, never stretched.
