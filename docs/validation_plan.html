Threadline validation plan

89 checks, run in order from one reset, about two hours. Each step says where to act, what to do click by click, the exact text to use, what you should see, what it validates and why that matters. The expected values came from running these flows on a throwaway copy of the desk with the same seed data, so your numbers should match if you follow the order. Parts marked with no exact number depend on what the live model replies.

What is left

How to play both sides on one phone. Your group chat is the team. Your private chat with the bot plays one workshop or client at a time: send /iam and tap a name. Each switch moves that party to the chat. Anything sent to a party that is not connected is saved, and offers arrive when you next become that workshop.

Where things are. The five tabs at the bottom are Today, Work, Messages, Network and Speak. Settings means the person icon at the top right of Today. Review is the link on a Today card. Slide means dragging the green button across.

What the colours mean. iPhone Private chat Group chat Mac say where to act. Your ticks stay in this browser.

?What is left

For the graded video: use the separate video script, which is the shorter test. This full plan is for learning how everything behaves. For TestFlight with your team: the app is close, and teammates need a way to reach the desk.

Do before recording

  • Run this plan once, on the real phone, with real Telegram. Note any step that fails, with its number.
  • Download a Premium voice. Only you can do this. It takes about a minute on a good connection.
  • Keep the Mac awake and on the hotspot. The desk, Whisper and the Telegram bot all run on it.

Proposed next, in order

  • Hindi order codes and some names. In test clips, spelled-out codes like ORD-041 and SteadyHands came back wrong in Hindi. Say order 41, and check Read as.
  • A fix for whole-number prices in Chinese. The unseen test found it, and it fails safely by asking again.

Needed for TestFlight with your team

  • A way for teammates to reach the desk. A secure tunnel to your Mac is enough, and the Mac stays on while they use it.
  • A TestFlight build. Without the timing probe, then a test of Apple's production push server.
  • App Store Connect. Create the app record and add your team as testers. Real sign-in and a public launch are not needed for this.

Known limits, no action needed

  • Workshops and clients are role-played. The professor has allowed this, and the app labels role-play on the record.
  • The Streamlit web desk still shows queues without the Completed rule. The phone and Telegram use the rule.
  • The evaluation results do not change. The evaluation has no stage events, so the Completed rule never runs in it.
  • The Network bar and the workshop name go to different pages. To tidy up in the next round of app changes.
  • A client notice check has one gap. It catches dates that are not on record, and would still allow the old back date.
